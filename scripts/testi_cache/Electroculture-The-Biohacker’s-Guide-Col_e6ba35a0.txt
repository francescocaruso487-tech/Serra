Tesla’s Forgotten Dream At the dawn of the 20th century, Nikola Tesla patented devices to capture “radiant energy”—free electricity falling from the sky like unseen rain. He believed it could power homes, heal bodies, and even transform agriculture. His dream was suppressed but not extinguished. Today, biohackers and visionaries are rediscovering Tesla’s insights—not through colossal towers, but through simple coils, rods, and antennas rising quietly from gardens. In 1927, French agronomist Justin Christofleau demonstrated potato harvests nearly doubled using nothing more than copper spirals embedded in soil. Forgotten for decades, his results are now resurfacing—inviting us to rejoin the silent circuit of Earth and sky.
